Order lifecycle and refunds

One checkout can contain orders from several sellers. Each seller order moves on its own, but they share one checkout.

TL;DR

NDA note: one checkout, one child order per seller, and idempotent refunds on a checkout those orders share.

01

Background

+

One checkout can contain orders from several sellers. Each seller order moves on its own (ship, deliver, cancel, return), but they share one checkout.

02

How it works

+
  1. Each seller gets a child order with its own items, address, fulfilment record, seller income record and status history.
  2. Paid flow: seller confirms, ships with tracking, marks delivered; the buyer confirms receipt or the order auto-completes after a window.
  3. Seller income is credited to the seller's balance only after a buffer period following completion.
  4. Cancellations: buyer request with seller approval, overdue cancellation by a scheduled job, or admin force-cancel.
  5. Returns: buyer requests, seller approves or rejects, buyer can escalate to admin; when goods come back, stock and any store credit are restored and the refund is issued.
  6. Store-credit refunds complete immediately; cash refunds stay pending until an admin confirms the transfer.
03

Design decisions

+
  • Every refund and restore step is idempotent, guarded by a status or timestamp check, so retries are safe.
  • Partial vs full refund on a shared checkout: the checkout is marked partially refunded while any other order in the same checkout is still paid.
  • Seller income on a timer, not at delivery, to leave room for returns; credited once, guarded by a timestamp, with lock ordering and deadlock retry.
  • Scheduled jobs never overlap.
